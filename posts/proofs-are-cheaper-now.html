Proofs Are Cheaper Now

November 2026 · Draft

Formal verification gives the strongest guarantee we know how to give. It was also tedious enough that almost nobody used it.

Writing a specification, then a proof that the code meets it, took specialist skill and a great deal of patience. The cost kept it to the places that could afford it.

The tedious part is the automatable part

Much of that effort is exactly what AI is good at: proposing proof steps, searching for lemmas, repairing a proof when the code changes. And formal verification has a property that makes it a natural partner for a fallible model. A proof checker doesn't care how a proof was found. It only cares that the proof is valid.

The model can be wrong as often as it likes. The checker only accepts what's true.

Why I think this is the future

If AI can write the code and also produce the proof, then reliability stops depending on human attention. I believe this is where software is heading, and it's what I want to spend my time on.
