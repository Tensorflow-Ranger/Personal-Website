What I'm betting on

01

Capable, not reliable

AI can write the code, the proof, the report. It can also be confidently wrong, and it won't tell you which.

02

Humans are the bottleneck

Review and supervision are how we earn trust today. They don't scale with a machine that produces work faster than anyone can read it.

03

Verify autonomously

Checks that run without a person: formal verification where we can state the property, and good proxy metrics where we can't.

04

AI makes proofs affordable

Formal verification used to be tedious enough to be rare. With AI automating large parts of it, I believe it becomes the default. That's the future I want to help build.
